Materials & limits

Clear expectations.
Better results.

We currently offer FDM printing in PLA and PETG. Resin printing, same-week service, sanding, painting, and other post-processing are not currently offered.

PLA

Clean & versatile

A dependable choice for display pieces, prototypes, props, and general-purpose printing.

PETG

Tough & practical

More heat-resistant and durable for functional parts and projects that need added resilience.

TIMING

Plan for 1–2 weeks

Exact timing depends on project scale, print duration, queue, and any required file preparation.

Before we print

Every file gets a practical review.

We check dimensions, orientation, likely support needs, material fit, and obvious printability risks before production begins.

  • Build-volume and scale check
  • PLA or PETG material guidance
  • Upfront timing and pricing
  • Progress communication during the job
