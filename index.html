How it works

Simple from
start to finish.

Most projects move from first conversation to delivery in about one to two weeks, depending on size and complexity.

01

Get in touch

Send an inquiry or book a free meeting so we can understand the project.

02

Share the idea

Provide your STL file or describe what you want to make. No file? We can discuss options.

03

Review & quote

We check scale, printability, materials, timing, and the 430 × 430 × 500 mm build area.

04

Print & ship

Your project is monitored, completed, and shipped to your address as printed.
