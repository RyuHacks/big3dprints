Huz Engineered / Big 3D Prints

It started with curiosity. It grew through care.

What began as a personal passion in 2022 became Big 3D Prints: a focused service from Huz Engineered, built on better equipment, smarter monitoring, and a commitment to getting every project right.

You will not be passed between departments or left talking to a bot. You work directly with the person preparing and printing your project, so questions are answered and adjustments are understood.

Our evolution

Always
improving.

Each upgrade has been driven by the same goal: more reliable printing and a better experience for every client.

2022

Workshop 1.0

The first printer came online in June and the foundation of the studio was set.

2023

Remote monitoring

Webcam support arrived in March, followed by the first stable multi-day print in August and a second printer in December.

2024

Capacity & intelligence

A third printer expanded capacity. AI-assisted failure detection, custom firmware, and mechanical upgrades improved reliability on taller prints.

2025

Workshop 5.0

A refined, upgraded setup shaped by three years of real projects, experimentation, and hands-on learning.
