Cookies & Privacy

A plain-language explanation of what this website stores, what the project form collects, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: August 30, 2026

On this page

1. Quick summary

Huz Engineered operates this website and the Big 3D Prints service. This website currently does not set first-party advertising or analytics cookies. It also does not use browser local storage to track visitors.

No cookie banner is shown because there are currently no optional tracking cookies to accept or reject. If non-essential cookies or similar tracking tools are added later, this notice and the available consent controls will be updated before they are used.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can place on a device. They can support essential functions, remember preferences, measure traffic, or support advertising.

The scripts used directly by this website provide navigation, page transitions, animations, and carousels without setting cookies. Depending on where the site is hosted, the hosting provider may process basic server logs or use strictly necessary technology for security and delivery. Server logs can include an IP address, browser type, requested page, date, and time.

3. Information you choose to provide

If you submit the project form, we may receive your name, email address, project description, uploaded 3D files, approximate timeline, postal code, previous printing experience, and your optional choice about anonymous social-media sharing.

If you contact us by email, book a meeting, purchase through Etsy, or otherwise communicate with us, we receive the information you provide through that channel. Please submit only files and personal information needed to assess the project.

4. How information is used

We use inquiry and order information to:

  • review files and determine whether a project is printable;
  • prepare quotes and communicate about requirements, price, timing, and delivery;
  • produce, document, and support accepted orders;
  • maintain business, transaction, tax, and dispute records where necessary; and
  • prevent misuse, protect the website, and comply with legal obligations.

Project photographs or videos are used publicly only when permission has been provided. We do not sell personal information.

5. Service providers and external websites

The project form is processed by Formspree. Form submissions and uploaded files are transmitted to Formspree so they can be delivered to us. Formspree may process information outside Canada under its own privacy terms.

Links to Google Calendar and Etsy take you to separate services. Those companies may use their own cookies and collect information according to their policies. Their cookies are not placed merely because a link appears on this website.

We may also use hosting, email, shipping, payment, accounting, or production providers where reasonably necessary to operate the service. We remain responsible for personal information under our control.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including completing a project, providing support, maintaining required records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Information that is no longer required is deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of where practical.

We use safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so avoid including unrelated confidential information in an uploaded file or message.

7. Access, correction, withdrawal, and questions

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or withdraw consent for an optional future use, subject to legal and contractual requirements. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already occurred with valid consent and may prevent us from completing a requested service.

For a privacy question or request, email huzaifa@big3dprints.ca. We may need to verify your identity before providing or changing personal information.

We may revise this notice if the website, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
